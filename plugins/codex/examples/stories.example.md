## Kafka migration under a deadline
Tags: leadership, ambiguity, technical depth, deadline

- **Situation:** Ingestion on the old queue was dropping events at peak; a large customer threatened to leave.
- **Task:** I owned getting p99 under one second before their renewal, eight weeks out.
- **Action:** Split the migration into shadow traffic, dual writes and cutover; wrote the replay tool myself; pushed back on a big-bang cutover the director wanted.
- **Result:** Cut over in week seven, p99 0.8 s, no lost events. The customer renewed for two years.
- **What I'd do differently:** Bring the customer's engineers in earlier; we lost a week guessing their peak shape.
